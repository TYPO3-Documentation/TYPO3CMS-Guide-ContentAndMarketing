============================
Guidelines for News Articles
============================

This guide helps authors create useful, accurate, and consistent articles suitable for
`news.typo3.com <https://news.typo3.com>`__.

For detailed rules on language, grammar, links, dates, numbers, quotations,
and capitalization, see the :ref:`Writing Style Guide <writing-style-guide>`.

For image licensing and attribution, see
:ref:`Images and Licensing <image-licensing>`.

For information about submitting an article and the publication process, see
:ref:`How to Get an Article Published in TYPO3 News <get-news-published>`.

.. note::

   These guidelines describe editorial principles. The `Content Group <https://typo3.community/contribute/teams-committees/marketing/content>`__
   may edit submitted content for clarity, accuracy, consistency,
   accessibility, legal compliance, or other legitimate editorial reasons.

.. _news-article-guidelines-checklist:

Quick Checklist
===============

Before submitting an article, you should be able to answer yes to all
of these statements (if applicable):

* The article has a clear purpose and provides useful information.
* The headline accurately describes the article.
* The article has a lead that explains the main news or purpose.
* The article provides the necessary context for readers who are unfamiliar
  with the topic.
* A relevant call to action or next step is included where appropriate.
* Facts, dates, names, numbers, and version information have been checked.
* Opinions, proposals, plans, and decisions are clearly distinguished.
* Quotes are accurate and attributed.
* External links provide a concrete benefit to readers.
* Link text is descriptive and avoids URLs in inline text.
* Images can legally be published and have complete licensing information.
* Required consent for recognizable people has been obtained.
* The article is not advertising or promoting commercial services.
* Sensitive and confidential information has been handled appropriately.
* The article follows the `TYPO3 Code of Conduct <https://typo3.org/coc>`__.
* The article follows the :ref:`Writing Style Guide <writing-style-guide>`,
  including US English spelling.
* AI-generated or AI-assisted content has been checked for accuracy.

.. _news-article-guidelines-what-is-news:

1. Defining TYPO3 News
======================

TYPO3 News is the shared platform for news and events publication for the
TYPO3 ecosystem: TYPO3 CMS, the TYPO3 community, teams, initiatives,
events, and other activities relevant to TYPO3.

The TYPO3 Association and TYPO3 Company are the responsible publishers.

TYPO3 News is an editorial publication, not an advertising platform. Its
purpose is to inform readers, document activities, share knowledge, and help
people understand and participate in the TYPO3 ecosystem.

.. _news-article-guidelines-contributors:

2. Who Can Contribute
=====================

Anyone involved in the TYPO3 community can submit article ideas and content.
You do not need to be a member of a particular team or have a long contribution
history.

If you have useful information but do not feel confident writing the article
yourself, ask for editorial support.

Possible support includes:

* Developing an article from an idea
* Copyediting and improving readability
* Proofreading
* Translating content into English

.. important::

   Authors remain responsible for providing accurate information and for having
   the necessary rights and permissions for submitted material.

.. _news-article-guidelines-what-we-publish:

3. What We Publish
==================

TYPO3 News articles cover topics such as:

* News about TYPO3 CMS development and releases
* Reports from TYPO3 teams, units, and initiatives
* Community news and community projects
* Event announcements and event reports
* Solutions to problems and other useful technical content
* Interviews and profiles
* Governance and organizational news
* Announcements from the TYPO3 Association or TYPO3 Company
* Stories about people contributing to TYPO3
* Relevant developments in open source, web development, and related
  technologies

..  tip::

    Are you are unsure whether the topic is suitable? Submit your idea already before
    writing a full article. See :ref:`How to Get an Article Published in TYPO3 News
    <get-news-published>`.

.. _news-article-guidelines-audience:

4. Audience
===========

TYPO3 News has an international audience with different levels of technical
knowledge.

Write for readers who may be:

* Experienced TYPO3 developers
* TYPO3 editors and integrators
* Agency or business professionals
* Community members
* People discovering TYPO3 for the first time
* People interested in open source and web technologies

Do not assume that every reader knows the history or context. Provide
enough information for the reader to understand why the article matters.

Explain specialized terms when they are important to understanding the
article. Link to useful explanations where appropriate.

.. _news-article-guidelines-editorial-principles:

5. Editorial Principles
=======================

TYPO3 News content should be:

**Useful**
   Give readers information, knowledge, context, or a clear way to take
   action.

**Factual**
   Distinguish facts from opinions, plans, proposals, and expectations.

**Transparent**
   Clearly identify the organization, team, project, or person responsible
   for statements and decisions. With very few exceptions, each article is
   published under a *single* writer's full name (byline).

**Accessible**
   Write so that readers with different backgrounds can understand the
   main message.

**Respectful**
   Follow the `TYPO3 Code of Conduct <https://typo3.org/coc>`__ and treat
   people, organizations, and communities fairly.

**Non-sensational**
   Avoid exaggerated claims, clickbait, artificial urgency, and unsupported
   superlatives.

**Editorial, rather than promotional**
   Articles should inform readers rather than primarily promote a product,
   service, company, event, or person.

.. _news-article-guidelines-article-types:

6. Types of Articles
====================

Different topics benefit from different formats. Choose the format that best
serves the reader.

**News or announcement**
   Reports something new, such as a release, decision, organizational
   change, or important development.

**Community report**
   Describes an activity, project, meeting, sprint, or initiative and its
   results.

**Event announcement**
   Provides useful information about an upcoming event and explains why it
   is relevant to TYPO3 readers.

**Event report**
   Summarizes what happened at an event, including relevant outcomes,
   contributions, and next steps.

**Best practice or how-to**
   Explains how results were accomplished with reference to a concrete
   case and problem. Generic tutorials belong in the documentation.
   Case studies are not published as TYPO3 News.

**Interview or profile**
   Presents a person, team, or project through questions, answers, or a
   structured profile.

**Opinion**
   Presents an individual's perspective or argument. Make it clear that the
   content represents an opinion rather than an official TYPO3 position.

**Governance or organizational article**
   Explains decisions, processes, structures, policies, or other
   organizational developments. Be precise about what was proposed,
   discussed, decided, or implemented.

.. _news-article-guidelines-structure:

7. Structure of an Article
==========================

A typical news article works well with the following structure:

**Headline**
   Clearly states what the article is about.

**Lead**
   Summarizes the most important information and gives the reader a reason
   to continue. Capped at 2–3 short sentences, it is also displayed in news
   lists.

**Context**
   Usually 1–3 paragraphs before the first section heading. Contains information
   needed to understand the context and background of the article.

**Main content**
   Split the content into descriptive section headings followed by 2–4 paragraphs
   of text.

**Next steps**
   Explains what happens next, when this is relevant to the story.

**Call to action**
   If readers can take a useful action, such as registering for an event,
   reading documentation, joining a discussion, or testing a release, tell
   them how. If the purpose is clear already at the beginning of the article,
   a call to action link can also be inserted between *Context* and *Main*
   *content*.

.. tip::

   Use headings to break up longer articles. Cap paragraphs at 2–3 sentences
   and give each paragraph one main idea. Do not add information just to make
   the article longer.

.. _news-article-guidelines-headlines-leads:

8. Headlines and Leads
======================

A headline should tell readers what the article is about without requiring
additional context.

Prefer specific headlines over vague or promotional ones.

**Good:**
   TYPO3 14.3.5 and 13.4.33 Security Releases Published

**Avoid**
   Important News You Don't Want to Miss

A lead should communicate the central news quickly. It should normally
answer the most important questions of what happened, who is involved, and
why it matters.

Do not repeat the headline word for word in the lead.

Avoid beginning with generic statements such as "We are excited to
announce..." unless the wording is genuinely relevant to the story.

Follow the :ref:`Writing Style Guide <writing-style-guide>` for title
capitalization and other language rules.

.. _news-article-guidelines-tone:

9. Tone and Voice
=================

Use a clear, professional, and approachable tone.

Prefer:

* Short, direct sentences
* Active voice where it improves clarity
* Concrete descriptions
* Plain language
* Specific names and dates
* Factual statements supported by evidence

Avoid:

* Marketing language
* Unnecessary jargon
* Exaggerated claims
* Vague corporate language
* Excessive use of exclamation marks
* Artificial urgency
* Language that assumes the reader already agrees with the author

All articles must be written in English. TYPO3 uses American (US) spelling.
See the :ref:`Writing Style Guide <writing-style-guide>` for detailed rules.

.. _news-article-guidelines-attribution:

10. Attribution and Organizational Identity
============================================

The shared news platform brings together content from different parts of
the TYPO3 ecosystem. Readers must be able to distinguish between them.
Use the specific name of an organization, team, or project when it matters.

For example:

* "The TYPO3 Association Board approved ..."
* "TYPO3 Company announced ..."
* "The Core Team released ..."
* "The Security Team published ..."
* "The TYPO3 Association is asking members to ..."

Avoid statements such as "TYPO3 decided ..." when the decision was actually
made by a specific body.

Do not imply that a personal opinion represents the position of anyone else
unless you are authorized to speak on their behalf.

For governance articles, distinguish carefully between information that was:

* Discussed
* Proposed
* Submitted for review
* Approved
* Decided
* Implemented
* Planned

Name people accurately and use their current role when the role is relevant
to the article. Link names to their profile on my.typo3.org.

.. _news-article-guidelines-links:

11. Links
=========

Links should help readers understand the article or take a relevant action.

Follow the link-prioritization rules in the
:ref:`Writing Style Guide <links-prioritizing>`.

We prefer relevant TYPO3 web properties where they provide the appropriate
information, but can link to external sites when they provide a concrete
benefit to the reader.

Never use URLs in article text and prefer descriptive link text rather than
"click here", "this", or similar expressions.

Do not link to external content advertising commercial or paid products or
services unless an official TYPO3 endorsement exists.

.. _news-article-guidelines-images:

12. Images
==========

All articles have at least one accompanying image or illustration. If you're
unable to find one, it will be found for you during the publication process.

Images should support the article and provide useful visual context.

Only use images for which TYPO3 has the necessary rights to publish,
distribute, and, where applicable, modify the image.

For detailed licensing requirements, see
:ref:`Images and Licensing <image-licensing>`.

Refrain from overly editing or cropping images before submitting. Always include
the following information:

* Creator or copyright holder
* Source
* Concrete license
* Any required attribution

If people are recognizable in an image, make sure you have their consent
before submitting it.

.. _news-article-guidelines-quotes:

13. Quotes
==========

Use quotation marks only when restating a person's exact words or quoting
a text. Do not use quotation marks for names or titles.

Minor grammatical corrections or necessary contextual changes must follow
the rules for quotes in the :ref:`Writing Style Guide <quotes>`.

If a quote was supplied by another source, verify that you have the right
to reproduce it and identify the source where appropriate.

.. _news-article-guidelines-facts:

14. Facts and Sources
=====================

You are responsible for the factual accuracy of submitted content.

Distinguish clearly between facts and statements.

For example, prefer:

   "The TYPO3 Association announced that ..."

over:

   "TYPO3 is now the market leader ..."

unless the latter statement can be supported by a reliable source and is
relevant to the article.

Do not present rumors, internal discussions, proposals, or expectations as
facts.

Where a claim is important to the story, provide a source or link that
allows readers to verify it.

.. _news-article-guidelines-commercial:

15. Promotional and Commercial Content
======================================

Articles should focus on information that is useful to readers.

Generally, it is not permitted to place any advertisements in articles.
An exception is cases where an official TYPO3 endorsement exists.

The :ref:`Writing Style Guide <links-prioritizing>` also gives
directions on what commercial content can be linked from articles.

.. _news-article-guidelines-sensitive:

16. Sensitive Content
=====================

All contributors must follow the
`TYPO3 Code of Conduct <https://typo3.org/coc>`__.

Writers should keep their texts factual and neutral, and not seek to
create, encourage, or participate in conflicts or disputes through their
content.

Unless permission is given by the TYPO3 Association or TYPO3 Company,
articles about sensitive topics can only be published under bylines of
official TYPO3 representatives. These topics include:

* Conflicts and disputes
* Legal matters (including legal advice)
* Security incidents
* Internal or confidential information
* Information that has not yet been publicly announced

.. _news-article-guidelines-ai:

17. AI-Assisted Writing
=======================

AI tools may be used to help develop, translate, edit, or proofread an
article, but they do not replace editorial responsibility. Authors remain
responsible for their content.

Do not publish AI-generated claims without checking them against reliable
sources. AI tools can produce plausible but incorrect names, dates, quotes,
statistics, references, and technical information.
